# Evidence Rules

These rules apply when the storyboard supports factual or financial claims. They govern how a visual is described and handed off; they do not replace legal, editorial, or platform-specific review.

## Identity and claim integrity

- A real report screenshot may be used as evidence only when its source and identity are recorded.
- Do not present a screenshot, logo, factory, office, chart, or person from one organization as evidence about another organization.
- If identity is uncertain, label the asset as uncertain and do not use it for an identity-dependent claim.
- Do not generate a plausible report page and present it as an authentic source document.

## Contextual B-roll

- Broadly relevant B-roll may support context when the shot does not claim that the depicted subject is the named entity.
- The storyboard should state when an image is illustrative or contextual rather than evidentiary.
- A related asset is not permission to imply ownership, location, partnership, or direct causation.

## Missing provenance

- Missing source, identity, or license information is a visible production issue, not a reason to silently substitute an asset.
- Record the issue in the asset candidate or manifest and let the workflow decide whether to block publication, replace the candidate, or use it only for internal preview.
